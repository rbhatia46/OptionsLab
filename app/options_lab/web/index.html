RESEARCH CONTRACT

Assumptions you can inspect.

1. Historical information only

Selection uses the latest eligible trade at or before the decision and a fresh futures price at that option tick. IV is solved from that observed premium, then Greeks are evaluated at the decision price and time. No fixed-volatility fallback is substituted for an invalid IV. The next available expiry at or after the DTE target is used, with at most a three-day difference.

2. Trade-print execution

Market sells consume prints with maker buyers; buys consume prints with taker buyers. Orders fill strictly after the decision plus latency, at a configurable fraction of observed volume. Each leg fills independently. Incomplete entries are unwound and retained in the ledger. Failed exits are kept as unresolved exposure, never silently removed. An unresolved position stops further entries and suppresses performance ratios.

3. Intraday scope & risk

One position per supported IST session. Stops and targets observe fresh as-of marks at each selected-option trade; portfolio delta exits also react to underlying ticks. Exit fills happen after the trigger, so realized losses can exceed a stop. All orders end before expiry. Unavailable exit liquidity is not replaced by a fabricated settlement. Long-dated contracts may be selected, but overnight positions, rolling, hedging, funding, and exchange liquidation are not simulated.

4. Greeks are model estimates

European Black–Scholes, ACT/365, zero dividend yield, configurable interest rate. The underlying is BTCUSD futures used as a spot proxy, not a true spot index or expiry-matched forward. Per-leg IV, delta, gamma, theta per day, and vega per volatility point are reported. Invalid, stale, or out-of-bound observations cannot qualify for delta selection.

5. Return & robustness reporting

The current UI charts cumulative USD P&L from zero and sizes positions in BTC. In BTC-size mode, daily P&L includes zero-trade dates; Sharpe uses √365 and a zero benchmark. Account percentage returns are omitted. Sortino uses downside deviation across daily P&L. Drawdown includes the initial capital peak and available intraday marks, with mark coverage shown. Missing observations can hide deeper losses. Sweeps choose a winner on development dates only; repeated use of the holdout makes it development data in practice.

6. Reproducibility

Every run stores the exact configuration, engine version, SHA-256 hashes and quality counters for the source CSVs, daily outcomes, legs, individual fills, risk paths, and skipped-entry reasons. Raw data is never modified. Fees and margin reserves are user-set research assumptions.
